engine-webgpu: GPU check

Runs the WebGPU network on the golden positions and compares it with the outputs captured from native KataGo; optionally measures evaluations per second by batch size. Search benchmark measures the search: visits per second on one position at batches 16, 32 and 64, and the time to review a fixed 250-move game at 100 visits a turn, one turn at a time at batches 64 and 16, and four turns at once at batch 16. Agreement positions records the search's recommended move on 50 positions of a fixed game at 100 and 500 visits, at batches 16 and 64, for comparison with native KataGo (scripts/native-agreement.mjs). A developer page, served by scripts/gpu-check.mjs or from the project's GitHub Pages site.


Without a file, the page uses the model in its model parameter, or else the test network. Goldens exist for kata1-b18c384nbt-s9996604416-d4316597426 and the test network.
