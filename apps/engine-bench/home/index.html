StoneMind is an open project to build an adaptive Go companion: one designed to learn how each player understands the game, how they choose to play and what they want to achieve, and to coach them toward the player they want to become.

StoneMind is still being built. We have set out our goals, and the work is happening in the open.

Read the charter Follow the work on GitHub

Our goals

  • Understand the game

    Through a Go ontology drawn from expert teaching.

  • Understand each player

    From their games, problems and study history.

  • Strengthen each style

    Telling a player's style apart from a weakness, and building on it.

  • Work toward the player's goals

    Goals the player defines, such as a rank, a tournament or a part of the game.

  • Keep players in charge

    Their data, knowledge and decisions stay theirs, kept on their own device by default.

  • Work with what players use

    Existing Go tools and the AI services a player chooses, in open formats.
