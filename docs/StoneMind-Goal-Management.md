# StoneMind Goal Management

Version: 0.1

---

# Philosophy

Players define success.

Not StoneMind.

The same player may have different goals at different times.

StoneMind adapts.

---

# Goal Types

## Improvement Goals

Examples:

- Reach 1 Dan
- Improve fighting
- Improve endgame

---

## Preparation Goals

Examples:

- Tournament preparation
- Club championship
- Teaching game preparation

---

## Opponent Goals

Examples:

- Improve against aggressive players
- Defeat a recurring rival

---

## Learning Goals

Examples:

- Understand influence better
- Learn invasions
- Understand professional games

---

## Exploration Goals

Examples:

- Experiment with influence-oriented play
- Learn new openings

---

# Goal Lifecycle

```text
Created
    ↓

Active
    ↓

Monitored
    ↓

Completed

or

Archived
```

---

# Goal-Aware Coaching

All recommendations should consider:

```text
Player Graph

+
Style Graph

+
Goal

+
Evidence
```

A recommendation is incomplete if it ignores goals.

---

# Goal Conflicts

StoneMind should identify conflicts.

Example:

Goal:
Play more aggressively

Goal:
Reduce risk

Potential conflict exists.

StoneMind should highlight it rather than silently choosing.

---

# Goal Success

Success should be measured by player-defined outcomes whenever possible.

Example:

Goal:
Understand professional games

Success:
Improved ability to explain professional decisions.

Not:

Rank increase.
